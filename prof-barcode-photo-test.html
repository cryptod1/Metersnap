OBSERVATION ≠ CERTIFICATION

Purpose

A supplier-neutral structured evidence record for meter work and future EV, heat-pump, solar/PV, battery, V2G and supply-upgrade workflows. Raw observations stay separate from Prof interpretation and from any authoritative verification.

1 · Asset identity

2 · Incoming supply evidence

3 · Existing low-carbon equipment

4 · Safety / tampering evidence

Conservative evidence only. Separate customer report, colleague observation and Prof visual evidence. Use “possible indication”; never accuse a person of tampering and never treat “nothing detected” as proof that the installation is safe.

5 · Evidence status

Photographic evidence should be dated and retained according to the approved operational/privacy policy. The passport records evidence; it does not certify installation suitability.

Data & integration map

Keep MeterSnap supplier-neutral and distinguish public information from authorised industry data.

Electricity

Identifier: MPAN. Expected asset data may be available through authorised EES/ECOES / ElectraLink routes, including supplier, MSN and technical meter details. These are controlled industry services, not unrestricted public customer-meter APIs.

Gas

Identifier: MPRN. Xoserve services can support authorised meter-asset data such as MSN, supplier, capacity, mechanism, type/model and manufacture information. Access is controlled.

Water

Identifier: SPID. A future supplier-neutral architecture can accommodate MOSL/CMOS where legitimate access and the use case permit it.

Matching principle

Authorised expected asset data and physical camera observations are different evidence sources. MeterSnap should compare them and surface mismatches; it must not silently turn one into the other.

Future integrations

Evidence may later support installer, DNO and ENA Connect Direct-style workflows for EV, heat pump, solar/PV, battery, V2G and supply upgrades. Suitability decisions remain with the authorised engineering process.

Scope boundary

Job Manager remains parked. No routing, allocation or scheduling is added to Prof evaluation or the Evidence Passport. MeterSnap stays focused on Scan → Record → Submit and reusable field evidence.
